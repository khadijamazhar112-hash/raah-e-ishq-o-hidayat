The Art of Quranic Tadabbur (تدبّر القرآن)

Allah says: "Do they not then reflect upon the Quran, or are there locks upon their hearts?" (47:24). Tadabbur is the personal engagement of the heart with the divine word—pondering the wisdom behind verses, assessing one's deeds in light of Allah's guidance, and turning insights into righteous character.
